### Philosophies

1) Optimization - done rarely, too much effort
	- algorithmic: are you doing n and not n2 where possible
	- look up CPUs math throughput
	- assess your code's latency using the previous two points
	- collect timings from CPU and compare to the assessment

2) Non-pessimization - the most missing
	- don't introduce unnecessary work, revise code for use of polymorphic stuff, virtual stuff, interpreting and so on

3) Fake optimization
	- fuck random hand-wave evidence. No memetic repeating. Only try and see the metric. The speedy things are super specific.
